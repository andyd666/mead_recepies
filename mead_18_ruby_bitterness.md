## Alcohol and sugar content

- Alc.: ~13.5%
- Sw.: 1.3g / 100ml

## Ingredients

- 3.5L boiled beet root juice
- 700g linden honey, caramelized
- ? sugar
- 2 boiled oranges
- 2g of yeast vitamins
- 2g of summer pollen
- 2g of spring pollen
- Enovini Honey, 16% yeasts

## Recipe

Caramelize honey (it was slightly burned). Boil 2 oranges, boil some amount of beet root, press juice from both oranges and beet roots. Let it cool down to 30C or less. Measure OG and add sugar so that OG is 1.120. Add vitamins, pollen and yeasts and let it ferment for some time

## Notes

Boiled oranges get super bitter taste. For me - it was unpleasant, and if some day I'd try to replicate it - freshly squeezed orange juice will work better. If you like bitter taste (coffee, beer, ~~talking to my ex~~) - it may work out, as it is not overly bitter
