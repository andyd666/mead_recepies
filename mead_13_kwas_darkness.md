## Alcohol and sugar content

- Alc.: ~14.0%
- Sw.: 1.3g / 100ml

## Ingredients

- 1200g of buckwheat honey
- 300g of rye malt
- 1 lime
- [Fill up to] 3L of water
- Tea
- 1.5g of yeast vitamins
- 1.5g of summer pollen
- Lalvin 71B (Fake) yeasts

## Recipe

Boil malt. Squeeze juice from lime and add to a water from malt. Make some black tea in a water, that will be added to a malt. Wait until it cools down to 30C or lower, add yeasts and let it ferment.

## Notes

It is not 3L of water, it was enough water to fill the 3L jar.
